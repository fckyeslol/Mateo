Leadership & Service

Throughout my education I have embraced leadership roles and sought to empower my peers. From founding clubs to mentoring younger students, I believe in the power of community and collaboration.

Clubs & Initiatives

In 9th grade, identified a gap in extracurricular life at my institution and took the initiative to found the Debate, Philosophy, and Model United Nations club — building it entirely from scratch. Beyond recruiting members and structuring the club's academic programs, I designed and developed the club's official website, creating a digital home that gave the organization visibility and helped attract new members across grade levels.

Representation & Advocacy

Elected by my peers to serve as student representative on the Board of Directors at IED Alexander von Humboldt, where I sat alongside faculty and administrative leadership to ensure student voices were heard at the highest institutional level. Simultaneously held the role of Student Comptroller, where I monitored resource allocation, promoted fiscal transparency, and advocated for policies that directly addressed student needs. These dual roles demanded maturity, diplomacy, and a genuine commitment to the community I represented.

PEP Program

Successfully completed the PEP Program, a rigorous initiative focused on educational development and community impact. Through this experience, I directly influenced the academic paths of more than 60 students from a boarding school, contributing to a program that goes beyond the classroom to open real opportunities for young people who might not otherwise have access to them.

Coaching & Mentoring

Served as coach and mentor across debate teams, Model UN delegations, and philosophy workshops, working closely with students to sharpen their logical reasoning, argumentation, and public speaking abilities. My approach focused not just on winning competitions, but on building lasting intellectual confidence and communication skills that students carry beyond any single event. In parallel, I guided peers through hackathon projects and innovation challenges, helping teams translate raw ideas into structured solutions while fostering a culture of teamwork, experimentation, and creative risk-taking.

Ortus Siderum Humboldt (OSH)

Assumed leadership of Ortus Siderum Humboldt (OSH), an astronomy and sciences initiative that I grew into a competitive force at the national level. Under my guidance, more than 20 students advanced to the national final round, a testament to the rigorous preparation, dedication, and sense of purpose the program cultivated. Most notably, two students earned the opportunity to compete at IOAA 2025 — one at the Junior level and one at the Senior level — representing the highest achievement the program has reached and a milestone I am deeply proud to have been part of.
